Open Cabwork How it works

Stop waiting on the cabinet shop.

I used to work at a cabinet shop. Contractors called all day asking for a design, a 3D picture, a cabinet list. The shop had the software, so the shop was the gatekeeper, and you waited.

That software is slow, dated and priced for showrooms. Cabwork does the same job in your browser, built so you can do it yourself.

  • Draw the room

    Pick a room shape or type in your tape-measure numbers. Add the doors and windows.

  • One click lays it out

    Quick generate fills the room with a kitchen. Move, swap or delete anything you don't like.

  • Show the customer

    Save 3D pictures of their kitchen for the bid. Offering 3D design is something you can advertise.

  • Send the cabinet list

    Print it or export it to Excel, and send it to as many shops as you like.

A cabinet list any shop can quote.

  • No prices, no waiting

    The list comes straight off your design: SKU, description, quantity and your notes, with the construction, door style, finish, hardware and crown on top.

    Send the same list to several cabinet shops and compare what comes back.

  • En español también

    The whole program is in Spanish, with a guided tutorial that builds a kitchen step by step.

  • Keep your kitchens

    Each job saves in your browser and as a project file you can reopen. Saving to an account is coming.
